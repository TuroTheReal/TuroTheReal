**Infrastructure** for startups, agencies and dev teams:

- **Monitoring**: dashboards, alerts that matter, logs and traces, handed over to your team
- **Go-live and hosting**: production image, automated TLS, verified backups, runbook
- **Hosting migration**: app by app cutover, rollback ready at every step
- **DevOps audit**: one day on your production without changing anything, written report ranked by severity
